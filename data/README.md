Dataset summary
- 5,901 order line items, Jan 2019 – Dec 2020
- 4 regions, 3 categories, 17 sub-categories
- Fields: order/ship dates, customer, segment, region, category, sub-category, sales, quantity, profit, returns, payment mode, shipping days, profit margin
Limitations
No discount, product cost, shipping expense or return-reason fields.
